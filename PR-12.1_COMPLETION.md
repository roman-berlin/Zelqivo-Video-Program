### Technical Details

**Theme Persistence:**
- Setting key: `appearance/theme`
- Values: `"light"` (default) or `"dark"`
- Stored in QSettings (platform-specific location)

**Keyboard Shortcut:**
- Ctrl+D: Toggle dark mode

**Color Schemes:**
- **Light Mode**: White/gray backgrounds, dark text, blue accents
- **Dark Mode**: Dark gray backgrounds (#2d2d2d), light text (#e0e0e0), blue accents

**Styled Components:**

- Toolbar and menubar integration
